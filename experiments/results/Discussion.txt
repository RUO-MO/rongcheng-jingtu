The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed dual-objective navigation framework effectively balances route distance and air quality exposure across diverse scenarios in Chengdu. As shown in Table 3, the weight sensitivity analysis reveals a clear trade-off: when $\alpha$=0 (greenest mode), the system selects routes through low-pollution areas such as the Panda Base and Du Fu Thatched Cottage, resulting in the lowest cumulative AQI exposure of 628.19 but a longer total distance of 95.57 km. Conversely, when $\alpha$=1.0 (shortest mode), the optimizer prioritizes direct routes regardless of pollution, achieving the minimum distance of 60.12 km but incurring the highest AQI exposure of 813.46. The distance variation across all $\alpha$ values is 35.45 km (59.0%), while the AQI exposure varies by 185.27 (29.5%), confirming meaningful route differentiation.

Table 4 further validates the practical benefit of health-aware routing: compared to the traditional distance-only approach ($\alpha$=1.0), the balanced mode ($\alpha$=0.5) reduces cumulative pollution exposure by 13.42% with a modest distance increase, yielding a health score improvement from 94.59 to 95.31. This finding is particularly relevant for vulnerable populations in Chengdu, where winter inversion layers frequently elevate PM2.5 levels in central districts.

From an optimization standpoint, the SCDP algorithm guarantees exact solutions for instances up to n=12 (Table 1), with average gaps of 2.58% against the greedy heuristic and 0.17% against the genetic algorithm. While the exponential complexity limits SCDP scalability beyond n=15, the GA provides near-optimal solutions within acceptable computation times (Table 2). The end-to-end latency analysis (Table 5) confirms that the dominant bottleneck is the API data retrieval (200-800 ms), with DP computation contributing less than 1% of total latency for n$\leq$10, supporting the practical feasibility of real-time deployment.

In summary, the dual-objective formulation with adjustable $\alpha$ provides a flexible decision-making tool that allows urban travelers to explicitly navigate the distance-pollution trade-off, a capability absent from conventional single-objective routing systems.
